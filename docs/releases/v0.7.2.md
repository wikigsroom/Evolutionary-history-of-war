# v0.7.2：AI 难度与首都进化

此前三档 AI 的金币预算相同，仅决策频率和失误率有区别；首都进化只保留残血比例。本版拉开 AI 经济能力，让双方各自在成功进化时按兵种生命倍率增长首都上限并恢复满血。

Previously, difficulty changed AI decision speed and mistake probability while gold budgets stayed equal. This release separates AI economies and makes successful evolution scale and fully heal that side's capital.

## 难度 / Difficulty

| 难度 / Difficulty | AI 开局金币 / Starting gold | AI 持续收入 / Passive income | 石器开局金币 / Stone Age gold | 石器金币/秒 / Gold per second |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 轻松 / Easy | 0.80× | 0.85× | 192 | 2.125 |
| 标准 / Standard | 1.25× | 1.35× | 300 | 3.375 |
| 挑战 / Challenge | 1.50× | 1.65× | 360 | 4.125 |

表中未叠加时代或构筑增益。玩家石器开局仍为 240 金币、基础收入 2.5/秒。战役在预设敌方金币上应用对应系数。决策间隔仍为 2／1／0.5 秒，失误率为 20%／8%／2%。难度按钮显示金币系数；经验仍通过战斗获取，没有被动经验或免费追赶升级。

Values exclude era/build modifiers. Player Stone Age gold stays at 240 and passive income at 2.5/s. Campaign enemy budgets receive the selected multiplier. Decision intervals remain 2/1/0.5 seconds and mistake rates 20%/8%/2%. Difficulty tooltips disclose the economic multipliers. XP remains combat-earned, without passive XP or free catch-up evolution.

## 首都 / Capital

成功支付本方进化经验后，本方首都最大生命按兵种生命倍率增长，当前每代 5×：2400 → 12000 → 60000 → 300000，依此类推。保留本方遗物、天赋及关卡生命增益，随后生命恢复到新上限。

双方升级独立，对方首都不会跟随回血或进化。指挥官继续保留生命比例与技能冷却；旧兵和付费训练订单保留出生时代。经验不足、时代封顶或首都已摧毁时，失败的升级不回血。加载残血存档也不会回血或再次领取开局金币。

Successful paid evolution scales that side's capital HP with troop HP, currently 5× per era, including equipped and mission bonuses, then fully heals it. The opposite capital is unaffected. Commander HP fraction/cooldowns and existing troop/order eras remain preserved. Failed, capped or post-destruction evolution cannot heal; loading a save neither heals nor reapplies starting gold.

## 验证 / Validation

- **187/187** 难度与首都专项检查；**420/420** 核心规则；**45/45** 旧存档检查。
- 三档难度各三个固定种子，旧版与新版各九场、最多 600 秒的对照。使用同一自动玩家策略，H01 对 H03，关闭随机事件。
- 新版轻松 AI 两场在第一时代被击败；标准 AI 两场进入第三时代，一场在第二时代被击败；挑战 AI 三场都存活到 600 秒并进入第三时代。旧版挑战三场在此窗口均停留第二时代。
- 一场扩展挑战样本存活至 1500 秒，在 1342.5 秒进入第六时代。未结束样本记录为存活，不计为 AI 获胜；这些样本不代表真人胜率。

187 focused checks, 420 core-rule checks and 45 legacy-save checks pass. Nine before/after samples use three seeds per difficulty against one fixed scripted player, with random events disabled. Challenge reaches era III in all updated 600-second samples, versus era II before. An extended sample reaches era VI at 1342.5 seconds and survives the 1500-second window. Unfinished samples are censored, not counted as AI victories; this is not a human win-rate study.
