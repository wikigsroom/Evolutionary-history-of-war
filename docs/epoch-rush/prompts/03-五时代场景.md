# 五时代场景与基地

每时代四项：基地一张、far/mid/ground三层背景。使用一致摄像机、光向和地面高度，不能把整张概念画作为不可交互战场。

材料参数：rough pale stone, leather, bone, wood stakes, skin banners, open warm valley。

Warm hand-drawn cel shading, bold dark-brown contour, two or three flat shading levels, clean chunky silhouette, matte painted materials. Friendly blue double-tail banner accents, warm parchment and ochre palette. Fully original character and props. Strict orthographic side view, facing RIGHT, full subject visible with generous margins. Full silhouette, flat front-side profile, large readable entrance, exactly three clear roof mounting pads for separate turrets, no mounted turret baked in, true transparent background, full bottom edge visible. Normalize to 512x512.
