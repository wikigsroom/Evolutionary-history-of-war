# 风格母版与执行规格

每条生产提示词由本母版、对象描述、尺寸/背景条件组成。素材ID与文件映射以asset-manifest为准。此目录提供提示词规格，尚未调用生成所有生产素材。

```text
Original 2D side-view historical cartoon game asset for Epoch Rush. Warm hand-drawn cel shading, bold dark-brown contour, two or three flat shading levels, clean chunky silhouette, matte painted materials. Friendly blue double-tail banner accents, warm parchment and ochre palette. Fully original character and props. Strict orthographic side view, facing RIGHT, full subject visible with generous margins. No lettering, logo, watermark, cinematic composition, photorealism, isometric camera, realistic gore or tiny decorative clutter.
```

角色透明背景条件：isolated on a true transparent RGBA canvas, no scenery, no cast-shadow baked outside the feet, readable at 80 pixels tall。必须检查真实alpha，网格图案不等于透明。

技能/遗物图标条件：one clear object only, centered, strong silhouette, no text, readable at 32 pixels, 128x128 normalized output。

原始请求通常1024x1024或横向1536x1024，高质量PNG；交付尺寸由本地裁切/缩放得到，背景2048宽可经本地扩展拼接后验接缝，不假设接口支持任意图集尺寸。默认模型gpt-image-2.5，实际调用固定指定脚本。
