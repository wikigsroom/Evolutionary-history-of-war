```text
Original 2D painted historical cartoon game icon, [SUBJECT], one centered clear object, chunky dark-brown contour, warm parchment/ochre and blue accents, two flat shading levels, true transparent background, no letters, digits, logo or decorative microdetail, legible at 32 pixels.
```
